    Questions sellers ask

    Straight answers.

    Do I need a developer or a designer?

    No. You add products, pick a colour and your shop is live. If you want more control later, you can change the theme.

    Can my customers pay with bKash and Nagad?

    Yes, along with cards and cash on delivery. Each method can be switched on or off in your settings.

    I already sell on Facebook. Do I have to stop?

    No. Keep posting. Put your shop link in the post, and orders from Facebook, WhatsApp and your shop all land in one list.

    What happens when something is almost out of stock?

    You set a reorder level for each product. When stock drops to it, you get a message, and the product shows how few are left.

    Can I use my own domain?

    Yes, on the Growth plan and above. We give you a free certificate so the padlock shows.

    Can I leave and take my data?

    Yes. Download your products, orders and customers as spreadsheet files whenever you like.

    Is it really free to start?

    The Starter plan costs nothing and has no time limit. You only pay when you need more than it includes.
